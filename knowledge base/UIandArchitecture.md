# Our Architecture and UI

**UI**

![image.png](attachment:279315b7-200c-4791-a796-82d17bd44c7a:image.png)

**Senior's App (4 screens):**

- Home — vitals at a glance, next medication, call family button
- Medication Tracker — per-pill status, low stock warning, auto-refill notice
- AI Health Call — incoming Twilio voice call from the AI agent
- Emergency Alert — critical vitals shown, SOS buttons for senior to respond

**Family's App (3 screens):**

- Family Dashboard — parent's live health summary + today's activity timeline
- Alerts — color-coded critical / warning / info notifications with call-to-action
- Weekly Report — AI-generated summary + charts for sleep, steps, adherence

**Web Dashboard (desktop):**

- Full sidebar nav, 4 metric cards, live alert feed with one-click "Call Now"
